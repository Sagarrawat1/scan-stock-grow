# ShopEase Smart

ScanSmart

Scan. Manage. Sell. Grow.

Smart inventory management made simple for every shopkeeper.

ScanSmart is a smart and easy-to-use inventory and sales management platform designed to help shopkeepers manage their products, track stock, monitor expiry dates, record sales, and understand their business performance — all in one place.

Simply scan a product barcode, and ScanSmart helps you register and manage the product in your digital inventory.

🚀 What is ScanSmart?

Managing products manually can be time-consuming and can lead to mistakes, expired stock, and difficulty tracking sales.

ScanSmart solves this problem with barcode-based inventory management.

Shopkeepers can scan a product's barcode to quickly add it to their inventory, store important product information, track quantities, monitor expiry dates, and record sales when products are sold.

Everything is organized in one simple dashboard.

⏰ Expiry Tracker

Never miss an expiring product.

Expired products can cause unnecessary losses.

ScanSmart automatically monitors product expiry dates and organizes products according to how soon they will expire.

Products expiring soon appear at the top.

The system can highlight products that are:

🔴 Expiring in less than 1 month

🟠 Expiring soon

🟢 Safe / longer expiry

A days remaining counter makes it easy to understand exactly how much time is left.

For example:

Milk Powder
Expires in 18 days

Biscuits
Expires in 7 days

Juice
Expires in 2 days

This helps shopkeepers identify products that should be sold first and reduce losses caused by expired stock.

💰 Profit & Loss Calculator

Know where your money is going.

ScanSmart can calculate your profit by comparing the price you paid for a product with the price at which you sold it.

Profit per product = Selling Price − Purchase Price

For example:

Purchase Price: ₹50
Selling Price: ₹70
Profit: ₹20
